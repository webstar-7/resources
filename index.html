Add NYANSATEK to your home screen

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom (or top) of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Open NYANSATEK from your home screen from now on.

Use Safari for this step: Chrome on iPhone may not show “Add to Home Screen”.
